Privacy Policy - Next Services

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Next Services is developed by Martin Johnston as an independent app. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Location

With your permission, your location is used on your device to show nearby services, sort by distance, and plan routes. Your location is never sent to our servers or stored by us.

Favourites and settings

Your favourite stations and preferences are stored on your device only.

Fuel price data

The app downloads fuel price and service data from our content delivery network (Cloudflare). Standard technical logs (such as IP address) are processed by Cloudflare to serve these requests; we do not use them to identify you.

Analytics

We use PostHog (hosted in the EU) to collect anonymous usage statistics, such as which screens are used, to help improve the app. This is not linked to your identity, and no advertising identifier is used for analytics.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows banner adverts served by Google AdMob. Google may process device information and identifiers to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's advertising policies.

Your choices

You can disable location access in iOS Settings at any time. You can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security.

Contact

Questions about this policy: NextServicesUK@gmail.com.
